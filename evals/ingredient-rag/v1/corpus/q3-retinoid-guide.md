# Synthetic guide to retinoids and retinol

## Retinoid or retinol: the difference

In this synthetic guide, retinoids are a group of vitamin A related ingredients, and retinol is one type of retinoid found in many cosmetic products. The guide says prescription retinoids are generally stronger than retinol products sold without a prescription.

## Starting gradually

The synthetic guide suggests starting a retinoid or retinol product gradually, applying it on only some nights at first and increasing use only as the skin tolerates it.

## Irritation caution

The synthetic guide warns that retinoids and retinol can cause irritation, including dryness, peeling, and redness, especially when someone first starts using them. However, the guide says this irritation often lessens as skin adjusts, and it advises stopping and asking a dermatologist if irritation is severe.

## Sun protection while using retinoids

The synthetic guide also recommends daily sunscreen while using retinoids, because it reports that skin may be more sensitive to the sun during use.

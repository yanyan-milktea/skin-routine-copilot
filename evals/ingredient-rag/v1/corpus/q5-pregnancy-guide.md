# Synthetic guide to skin care during pregnancy

## General scope

This synthetic guide offers general educational information about skin care routines during pregnancy and breastfeeding. It notes that many people change their routine during pregnancy and that individual needs differ.

## Talk with a clinician

The synthetic guide advises anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding to talk with a clinician, such as an obstetrician or dermatologist, before starting or stopping skin care ingredients.

## Ingredients to discuss

According to the synthetic guide, retinoids are among the ingredients people are commonly advised to avoid during pregnancy, and products with salicylic acid are ones to discuss with a clinician. The guide does not list every ingredient and does not say any product is safe for a specific person.

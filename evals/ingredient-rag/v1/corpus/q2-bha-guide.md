# Synthetic guide to beta hydroxy acids

## What beta hydroxy acids are

In this synthetic guide, salicylic acid is the beta hydroxy acid (BHA) most often found in cosmetic products. The guide notes that salicylic acid is chemically related to alpha hydroxy acids but is a different ingredient.

## Cosmetic or drug

The synthetic guide explains that a salicylic acid product may be a cosmetic or a drug depending on its intended use. A product intended to treat acne is described as a drug in this fictional system, while a product intended only to cleanse or beautify is described as a cosmetic.

## Reviewed safety context

According to the synthetic guide, beta hydroxy acid products may also increase sun sensitivity, and some people may notice irritation such as stinging or redness. The guide recommends sun protection while using these products. It gives no safe concentration for any individual.

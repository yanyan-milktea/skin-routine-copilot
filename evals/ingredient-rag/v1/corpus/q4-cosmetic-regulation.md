# Synthetic note on cosmetic ingredient regulation

## Premarket approval

In this synthetic note, the fictional regulator does not require premarket approval for cosmetic products or their ingredients before they are sold. Except for color additives, the note says the fictional regulator does not pre-clear ordinary cosmetic ingredients.

## Color additive exception

The synthetic note explains that color additives are the exception: they must be approved by the fictional regulator for their intended use before they appear in a cosmetic, unless a listed exemption applies.

## The regulator's role

According to the synthetic note, the fictional regulator can take enforcement action against cosmetics on the market that are unsafe or mislabeled. The note says companies are responsible for making sure their products are safe before sale.

## Market availability is not approval

The synthetic note warns that being sold in stores does not mean a cosmetic or ingredient received premarket approval.
